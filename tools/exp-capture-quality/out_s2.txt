6. ARE THE MEASURED ALIGNMENTS REAL? (transitivity of registration)
==============================================================================
   For a triple i,j,k the shifts must compose: d(i,j)+d(j,k)=d(i,k).
   Random/meaningless alignments do not compose. Impostor triples,
   which cannot have a true registration, are the null distribution.
   [matcher window +-20/+-8 ] genuine  n= 4000  |closure error| med  23.0 px  (shuffled null  24.0)  exact-ish (<=4px):  3.1% (null  2.1%)
   [matcher window +-20/+-8 ] impostor n=  364  |closure error| med  24.0 px  (shuffled null  23.0)  exact-ish (<=4px):  2.7% (null  2.7%)
   [wide +-60/+-20 strict   ] genuine  n= 4000  |closure error| med  53.0 px  (shuffled null  58.0)  exact-ish (<=4px):  0.8% (null  0.5%)

9. RE-PRESS CEILING: what if the device simply asked for MORE presses?
==============================================================================
   Score a genuine 'attempt' as the max over m independent probe
   captures (the user presses up to m times, any accept wins). The
   impostor gets the same m attempts, so FAR rises too. This is the
   honest version of 'just retry', and it needs no quality metric.
   m=1 attempts:  d'= 1.83  EER= 15.0%  FAR@10%FRR= 14.6%
   m=2 attempts:  d'= 1.62  EER= 25.7%  FAR@10%FRR= 25.4%
   m=3 attempts:  d'= 1.56  EER= 19.8%  FAR@10%FRR= 35.9%
   (d' is roughly flat: retrying moves genuine and impostor
    distributions together, so it buys convenience, not security.)
